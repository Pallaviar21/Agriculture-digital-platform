Government Schemes

  • PM Kisan Scheme

    Provides income support of ₹6000 per year to all landholding farmer families to supplement their financial needs.

  • MGNREGA

    Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act offers 100 days of wage employment to rural households.

    Housing scheme aimed at providing affordable housing to the urban and rural poor of India.
